Academic Evaluation & Trade-off Analysis:

  • Backtrack Thrashing Elimination: Intelligent variable selection (MRV / "fail-first") and domain pruning (Forward Checking) reduce backtracks drastically compared to unguided row-major backtracking.
  • Computational Overhead Trade-off: AC-3 performs rigorous arc revisions ($X_i \ne X_j$) at each assignment. On small 4x4 boards, queue maintenance introduces constant-factor overhead. On large 9x9 boards, it scales exponentially better by detecting inconsistencies at the root.
